          What does this mean?

          Your money grows by being invested. That usually means funds that own shares in many companies, so it grows as those companies do. This is not interest from your bank.

          After inflation means after taking off rising prices. If your investments grow 8% in a year and prices rise 3%, you can really buy about 5% more. All the numbers here use that real growth, so they stay in today's money.

          These are rough long-term guesses, not promises. Investments go up and down, and some years they lose money. Money left in a bank account often only keeps up with prices, or falls behind them, so 0% is a fair guess for it.
